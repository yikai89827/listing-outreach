# Privacy Policy — Listing Outreach

**Last updated:** 2026-11-04

Listing Outreach is a Chrome extension for real-estate professionals.

## Data we collect

We do **not** operate a backend in v1. Listing details you capture, your profile, templates, follow-up notes, and license status are stored in **Chrome local storage on your device**.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your listings to train AI models.

## Permissions

- **storage** — save your profile, templates, and follow-ups locally  
- **sidePanel / activeTab / scripting** — show the UI and read the listing page you open  
- **Host access to zillow.com / redfin.com** — extract publicly visible listing fields from pages you visit
